## Ingredients

- 1 cup quinoa
- 2 cups water
- cherry tomatoes
- green onions
- avocado
- cucumber
- 2 tbsp tahini
- 5 tbsp lemon juice
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- salt and pepper
- seeds, to taste

## Instructions

1. Boil the quinoa with the water and a pinch of salt until the water is absorbed.
2. Dice the vegetables.
3. Mix the tahini, lemon juice and olive oil. Season with salt and pepper.
4. Toss everything together.
